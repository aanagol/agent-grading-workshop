# Northwind Cloud — known facts

Both support-drafter and support-grader should treat these as ground truth.
Anything not listed here is NOT a known fact — a reply that states it as one is inventing policy.

- Plans: Free (5GB), Plus ($6/mo, 200GB), Team ($12/user/mo, 2TB pooled).
- Refunds: full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. After 14 days, no refunds except for billing errors.
- File recovery: deleted files are recoverable from trash for 30 days, then permanently removed.
- Support hours: 24/5 (Mon-Fri) for chat, email support 7 days/week.
